# 第 26 课｜2.3 贫困、人口与发展障碍

所属大章：经济发展｜Economic development

## 知识点精讲

绝对贫困指基本需要难以满足；相对贫困强调与社会正常生活水平的差距。人口快速增长可扩大劳动年龄人口和市场，也可能压低人均资本、增加学校医疗负担与失业。低储蓄、基础设施不足、政治不稳定、初级产品依赖和债务均可形成发展陷阱。

## 深度展开与真题化应用

#### 贫困的两种口径

Absolute poverty 指收入不足以满足 food、shelter 等基本需要；relative poverty 指收入显著低于社会通常水平。低收入→营养教育不足→productivity 低→工资低，形成 poverty cycle。税收转移、教育医疗和就业增长可能打破循环，但政策有财政成本和激励问题。

#### 人口数量之外还有结构

Birth rate、death rate、life expectancy 和 net migration 改变人口规模；age structure 与 dependency ratio 影响 labour supply、tax revenue 和公共支出。年轻人口可能带来未来劳动力优势，也需要当前教育投入；老龄化增加养老金和医疗压力，但也可能扩大养老服务需求。

#### English development

Population growth can increase the labour force and market size, but it may reduce living standards if output, housing and public services do not keep pace. Migration affects both the size and skill composition of the labour force.

#### 真题化知识应用

讨论高 birth rate 时，不只写“人口多”：短期 children dependency rises→education/health spending rises；长期 labour force may grow→productive capacity and tax revenue rise。结论取决于 job creation、human-capital investment 和资源承载力。


## 逐题精讲与评价拓展

#### 进一步拆解：这一课真正考什么

贫困部分区分 absolute 与 relative；人口部分区分 total population、growth rate、age structure、labour force 和 dependency。二者通过 education、health、employment 与 public finance 相互作用。

#### 完整因果链｜Cause-and-effect chain

**low income -> poor nutrition/education -> low productivity -> low wages -> limited saving/investment -> poverty persists。政策可在 education、health、credit 或 job creation 处打断循环。** 写分析题时把每个箭头改成 because 或 therefore 连接的完整句子；如果中间一步无法解释，说明链条仍有跳跃。

#### 数字与情境示范｜Numerical or contextual example

工作年龄人口600万，儿童250万，老人150万，dependency ratio=(250+150)/600×100=66.7%。这不表示每100名工人实际供养66.7人，因为工作年龄者并非全就业。

#### English guided explanation

Absolute poverty concerns inability to meet basic needs. Relative poverty compares a household with prevailing living standards. A high dependency ratio may increase pressure on workers and public services, but dependants can also contribute unpaid work and future labour.

#### Cambridge-style multiple-choice application

**Question.** Which change directly increases an ageing dependency ratio: higher birth rate, longer life expectancy with low fertility, net immigration of young workers, or later retirement?

**Answer and reasoning.** Longer life expectancy with low fertility, because the elderly share rises relative to the working-age population. 选择题不仅要找到正确项，还要说明其余方向为什么与定义、公式或因果关系不符。

#### Cambridge-style structured application

**Question.** Discuss whether population growth benefits a developing economy.

**Model development.** It can expand labour supply, demand and tax base, encouraging investment. A young population can become a demographic dividend. But education, housing and jobs must expand; otherwise unemployment, congestion and pressure on resources may rise.

#### 高频混淆点｜Common misconception

低 birth rate 不必然使人口立即下降；migration、death rate 和 population momentum 也会影响。

#### 条件与评价｜Conditions and evaluation

人口结果取决于年龄结构、人力资本、女性就业、工作机会和基础设施，而非只看人数。 评价不是另起一个无关观点，而是改变时间、幅度、弹性、资源条件或利益相关者后，检查原结论是否仍成立。


## English explanation

Absolute poverty concerns basic needs; relative poverty compares income with social norms.

## Cambridge exam focus

大考口径：准确定义；紧扣题干应用；解释完整因果链；评价结论说明条件。
