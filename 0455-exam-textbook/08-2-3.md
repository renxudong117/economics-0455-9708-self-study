# 第 8 课｜2.3 需求量移动与需求曲线移位｜Movement along and shift of demand

所属大章：资源配置｜The allocation of resources

## 知识点精讲

商品自身价格下降会导致 **extension in demand / increase in quantity demanded**，表现为沿同一条需求曲线向右下移动；自身价格上升会导致 contraction。收入、相关商品价格、广告、偏好、人口结构、天气和未来价格预期属于非价格决定因素，它们改变整条需求曲线。正常品收入增加使需求右移；劣等品收入增加可能使需求左移。替代品价格上涨会增加本商品需求；互补品价格上涨会减少本商品需求。

**English explanation.** A change in the product's own price causes a movement along the existing demand curve. A non-price determinant causes the whole curve to shift. Write “quantity demanded increases” for an extension and “demand increases” for a rightward shift.

## 深度展开与真题化应用

#### 一条曲线还是两条曲线

判断的第一问永远是：变的是商品自己的价格，还是其他条件？Own price change 只改变 **quantity demanded**，点沿原曲线移动；non-price determinant 改变 **demand**，整条曲线移动。价格下降形成 extension in demand，价格上涨形成 contraction in demand。收入、偏好、广告、人口、预期和相关商品价格不在纵轴上，所以需要画新曲线。

#### 相关商品与收入的方向判断

咖啡与茶是 substitutes：咖啡价格上升，部分消费者改买茶，茶的 demand increases。汽车与汽油是 complements：汽油价格上升使驾车总成本增加，汽车需求下降。收入上升令 normal good 需求增加，却可能令 inferior good 需求减少。先识别关系，再写消费者选择改变，最后判断 D0→D1 的方向。

#### English development

An extension of demand is an increase in quantity demanded caused only by a fall in the product's own price. An increase in demand is a rightward shift caused by a non-price determinant. These expressions are not interchangeable. In written answers, name the determinant, explain the change in willingness and ability to buy, and state the direction of the shift.

#### 真题化知识应用

仿真选择题：The price of smartphones is unchanged, but mobile-data charges fall. Smartphone demand is likely to increase because the services are complementary。图上应保持 smartphone price 轴不变，把 D1 向右移到 D2。若手机自身降价，则只能在 D1 上由 A 移到 B。


## 逐题精讲与评价拓展

#### 进一步拆解：这一课真正考什么

先圈出变化变量。Own price 在纵轴上，变化造成 movement；income、taste、advertising、population、expectations 与 related goods prices 不在纵轴上，变化造成 shift。语言上 extension/contraction 对应 quantity demanded，increase/decrease 对应 demand。

#### 完整因果链｜Cause-and-effect chain

**tea price rises -> coffee is a substitute -> consumers switch from tea to coffee -> coffee demand increases -> coffee demand curve shifts right -> coffee equilibrium price and quantity tend to rise。** 写分析题时把每个箭头改成 because 或 therefore 连接的完整句子；如果中间一步无法解释，说明链条仍有跳跃。

#### 数字与情境示范｜Numerical or contextual example

手机价格由 $500 降至 $450，购买量 1 000 增至 1 200，是 extension of demand 200。若价格仍为 $500 而收入增加后购买量变为 1 300，则 demand increases，整条曲线右移。

#### English guided explanation

The curve represents the relationship between the product's own price and quantity demanded. Therefore, a change in any other determinant changes the relationship itself and requires a new curve. State both the determinant and why willingness or ability to buy changes.

#### Cambridge-style multiple-choice application

**Question.** The price of petrol rises. What happens to demand for petrol cars, assuming they are complements?

**Answer and reasoning.** Demand for petrol cars decreases and its demand curve shifts left. The car price need not have changed. 选择题不仅要找到正确项，还要说明其余方向为什么与定义、公式或因果关系不符。

#### Cambridge-style structured application

**Question.** Analyse how higher household income may affect demand for bus travel.

**Model development.** If bus travel is an inferior service, higher income enables households to switch to cars or taxis. Demand for bus travel falls and its curve shifts left. If buses are high-quality and environmentally preferred, they may instead be normal, so the result depends on consumer behaviour.

#### 高频混淆点｜Common misconception

“价格下降使需求增加”在 Cambridge 精确口径中应写 quantity demanded increases 或 extension in demand。

#### 条件与评价｜Conditions and evaluation

相关商品关系和 normal/inferior 属性不是永远固定，可能随收入水平、地区、品质与时间改变。 评价不是另起一个无关观点，而是改变时间、幅度、弹性、资源条件或利益相关者后，检查原结论是否仍成立。


## English explanation

Define the key term precisely, apply it to the case material and develop a complete economic explanation.

## Cambridge exam focus

大考高频辨析：own price change = movement along；non-price determinant = shift。必须正确使用 quantity demanded 与 demand。
