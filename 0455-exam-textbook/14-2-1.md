# 第 14 课｜2.1 货币与银行（Money and banking）

所属大章：微观经济决策者｜Microeconomic decision makers

## 知识点精讲

货币的四项功能是 **medium of exchange（交换媒介）**、**measure of value（价值尺度）**、**store of value（价值储藏）** 与 **standard of deferred payment（延期支付标准）**。商业银行（commercial bank）接受存款、提供支付服务并放贷；中央银行（central bank）通常发行货币、监管银行并影响利率。通胀削弱货币的购买力，因此“钱可以储藏价值”不等于“价值永远不变”。

## 深度展开与真题化应用

#### 货币功能与良好货币特征

货币作为 medium of exchange 消除 barter 的 double coincidence of wants；作为 measure of value 让商品可比较；作为 store of value 把购买力转移到未来；作为 standard of deferred payment 支持借贷。良好货币应 durable、portable、divisible、recognisable、scarce and stable in value。高通胀会削弱价值储藏功能。

#### 商业银行与中央银行

商业银行接受 deposits、提供 loans、支付和外汇服务，并通过利差盈利。中央银行通常发行货币、监管银行、作为政府银行和最后贷款人，并实施 monetary policy。利率上升提高借款成本，可能减少消费和投资；同时提高储蓄回报。结果取决于信心、债务类型和利率变化幅度。

#### English development

Commercial banks connect savers with borrowers and provide liquidity and payment services. A central bank is responsible for monetary stability and supervision. Do not confuse money with income or wealth: money is an asset used to make transactions, while income is a flow received over time.

#### 真题化知识应用

题目若问“Explain two functions of money”，每个功能要说明如何发挥作用。若问利率上升对家庭，既可写 mortgage repayments rise→disposable income falls→consumption falls，也可写 saving becomes more rewarding→saving rises。


## 逐题精讲与评价拓展

#### 进一步拆解：这一课真正考什么

先区分 money、income、wealth 和 credit。货币是被普遍接受的交易资产；收入是一段时间的流量；财富是资产存量；信用是借款能力。再区分 commercial bank 与 central bank。

#### 完整因果链｜Cause-and-effect chain

**central bank raises interest rate -> borrowing becomes dearer/saving more attractive -> household consumption and firm investment may fall -> AD falls -> inflationary pressure may ease。** 写分析题时把每个箭头改成 because 或 therefore 连接的完整句子；如果中间一步无法解释，说明链条仍有跳跃。

#### 数字与情境示范｜Numerical or contextual example

存款 $1 000、年利率3%，一年利息约 $30。若通胀5%，实际购买力仍可能下降约2%，说明 nominal return 与 real return 不同。

#### English guided explanation

Money performs four functions: medium of exchange, measure of value, store of value and standard of deferred payment. Commercial banks accept deposits and make loans. The central bank conducts monetary policy, supervises the banking system and may act as lender of last resort.

#### Cambridge-style multiple-choice application

**Question.** Which function of money is weakened most directly by rapid inflation?

**Answer and reasoning.** Store of value, because money loses purchasing power quickly over time. 选择题不仅要找到正确项，还要说明其余方向为什么与定义、公式或因果关系不符。

#### Cambridge-style structured application

**Question.** Analyse how a rise in interest rates may affect households and firms.

**Model development.** Loan and mortgage repayments may rise, reducing disposable income and consumption. Saving becomes more attractive. Firms face a higher cost of borrowing, so fewer investment projects are profitable. Demand and employment may fall, although savers receive more interest.

#### 高频混淆点｜Common misconception

中央银行通常不为普通家庭办理日常存款；商业银行也不负责制定全国政策利率。

#### 条件与评价｜Conditions and evaluation

利率传导取决于固定或浮动贷款比例、信心、负债水平和时滞，不能断言消费投资立即下降。 评价不是另起一个无关观点，而是改变时间、幅度、弹性、资源条件或利益相关者后，检查原结论是否仍成立。


## English explanation

Money is a medium of exchange, a measure of value, a store of value and a standard of deferred payment.
