# 第 12 课｜2.7 PED、PES 与销售收入｜Elasticity and revenue

所属大章：资源配置｜The allocation of resources

## 知识点精讲

PED = percentage change in quantity demanded ÷ percentage change in price。绝对值大于 1 为 elastic，小于 1 为 inelastic，等于 1 为 unit elastic。对缺乏弹性的需求，提高价格会使 total revenue 上升；对富有弹性的需求，提高价格会使 total revenue 下降。PES 取决于闲置产能、库存、生产周期和要素流动性。

**English explanation.** Price elasticity measures responsiveness, not the direction of a curve shift. Always show percentage changes and interpret the value in context. A firm facing elastic demand may raise revenue by lowering price.

## 深度展开与真题化应用

#### 弹性衡量“反应有多大”

PED 使用百分比而非绝对变化，使不同货币和数量单位可以比较。通常取绝对值分类：|PED|>1 elastic，|PED|<1 inelastic。必需品、替代品少、支出占比低以及短期通常使需求较缺乏弹性；奢侈品、替代品多和较长时间使需求较富弹性。PES 则取决于 spare capacity、stocks、production period 和 factor mobility。

#### PED 与 total revenue

TR=P×Q。若需求缺乏弹性，价格提高的百分比大于数量减少的百分比，TR 上升；若需求富有弹性，数量减少比例更大，TR 下降。计算时例如 P 由 10 增至 12（20%），Q 由 100 降至 70（30%），PED=30/20=1.5，属 elastic，TR 从 1000 降至 840。

#### English development

Price elasticity of demand measures the responsiveness of quantity demanded to a change in price. It does not explain why demand shifts. Price elasticity of supply measures how easily producers can change output when price changes. Interpretation must include both the numerical category and its meaning in context.

#### 真题化知识应用

选择题可能不要求算公式，而用 revenue 判断：price rises and total revenue falls implies elastic demand。问答题“Explain two causes of inelastic demand for rice”应发展 necessity、few close substitutes、small budget share 或 short time period，每点都说明消费者为何难以减少购买量。


## 逐题精讲与评价拓展

#### 进一步拆解：这一课真正考什么

弹性比较百分比反应。PED 通常为负，但分类常用绝对值；PES 通常为正。计算后必须用 elastic/inelastic/unit elastic 分类，并解释“价格变 1%，数量大约变多少”。

#### 完整因果链｜Cause-and-effect chain

**many close substitutes -> consumers can switch easily after price rise -> large percentage fall in Qd -> PED more elastic。spare capacity -> firms expand quickly after price rise -> larger percentage rise in Qs -> PES more elastic。** 写分析题时把每个箭头改成 because 或 therefore 连接的完整句子；如果中间一步无法解释，说明链条仍有跳跃。

#### 数字与情境示范｜Numerical or contextual example

价格由 $20 降至 $18，下降10%；Qd由200增至250，增加25%；|PED|=25/10=2.5，elastic。TR 由4000增至4500，符合降价提高富弹性需求下收入。

#### English guided explanation

Elasticity measures responsiveness, not slope alone. The same straight demand curve has different elasticity at different points. Determinants include substitutes, necessity, budget share and time; for supply, capacity, stock and production time are central.

#### Cambridge-style multiple-choice application

**Question.** Price rises by 8% and quantity demanded falls by 4%. What is PED and what happens to total revenue?

**Answer and reasoning.** PED is 0.5 in absolute value, so demand is inelastic and total revenue is likely to rise. 选择题不仅要找到正确项，还要说明其余方向为什么与定义、公式或因果关系不符。

#### Cambridge-style structured application

**Question.** Analyse why demand for a life-saving medicine may be price inelastic and how this affects a tax.

**Model development.** The medicine is a necessity and may have few substitutes, so patients reduce purchases by a smaller percentage than the price rise. A tax can therefore raise substantial revenue, but consumption and the related external cost may fall only slightly. Low-income patients may bear a large burden.

#### 高频混淆点｜Common misconception

弹性没有货币单位。不要把“2”解释为数量增加2个；应写 quantity changes by about 2% for a 1% price change。

#### 条件与评价｜Conditions and evaluation

企业收入结论要求其他条件不变。竞争者反应、品牌差异和成本变化可能让实际 revenue 与简单 PED 预测不同。 评价不是另起一个无关观点，而是改变时间、幅度、弹性、资源条件或利益相关者后，检查原结论是否仍成立。
