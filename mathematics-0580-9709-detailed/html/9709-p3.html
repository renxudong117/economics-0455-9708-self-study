第 1 课｜多项式与部分分式｜Polynomials and partial fractions

概念拆解｜Concept and meaning

因式定理、余式定理与多项式除法用于拆解高次式；部分分式把有理式化为可积分形式。

Detailed English explanation. Algebra records a sequence of equivalent statements. Show the operation that preserves equality, distinguish an expression from an equation, and check restrictions caused by denominators, logarithms, roots or modulus. Substitute solutions back into the original statement.

原理与方法｜Reasoning and method

先确认真分式；分母不同因子决定分子形式，重复因子需逐级列项。

例题一｜Worked example

分解 (5x+1)/[(x+1)(x−2)]。

解：令=A/(x+1)+B/(x−2)，得5x+1=A(x−2)+B(x+1)。代x=2得B=11/3；x=−1得A=4/3。

Cambridge-style application

Find remainder when p(x)=x³−2x+5 is divided by x−2.

Solution: Remainder=p(2)=8−4+5=9.

第 3 课｜三角函数深化｜Advanced trigonometry

概念拆解｜Concept and meaning

辅助角把asinx+bcosx化成Rsin(x+α)；恒等变换为方程和最大最小值服务。

Detailed English explanation. Use more than one representation. A formula gives exact relationships, a graph shows shape and intersections, and a table reveals numerical patterns. Factorised, expanded and completed-square forms each expose different information.

原理与方法｜Reasoning and method

R=√(a²+b²)，比较系数求α，随后处理值域。

例题一｜Worked example

写 3sinx+4cosx 为 Rsin(x+α)。

解：R=5，5cosα=3，5sinα=4，所以α=tan⁻¹(4/3)。

Cambridge-style application

Hence state the maximum value.

Solution: Since sin(x+α)≤1, the maximum is 5.

第 4 课｜复杂微分｜Further differentiation

概念拆解｜Concept and meaning

隐函数、参数方程和二阶导数扩展变化率分析。

Detailed English explanation. Calculus links local change with accumulated change. Identify the inner function or variable, retain the constant of integration when required, and interpret derivatives, stationary points and definite integrals rather than treating them as symbol manipulation.

原理与方法｜Reasoning and method

隐式微分每个y项乘dy/dx；参数式dy/dx=(dy/dt)/(dx/dt)。

例题一｜Worked example

x²+y²=25，求dy/dx。

解：2x+2y dy/dx=0，所以dy/dx=−x/y。

Cambridge-style application

Given x=t²+1,y=t³, find dy/dx.

Solution: dy/dx=(3t²)/(2t)=3t/2, for t≠0.

第 6 课｜微分方程｜Differential equations

概念拆解｜Concept and meaning

一阶可分离微分方程描述变化率随变量变化；积分常数由初始条件确定。

Detailed English explanation. Algebra records a sequence of equivalent statements. Show the operation that preserves equality, distinguish an expression from an equation, and check restrictions caused by denominators, logarithms, roots or modulus. Substitute solutions back into the original statement.

原理与方法｜Reasoning and method

把y项与x项分到两边，分别积分，再代初值。

例题一｜Worked example

dy/dx=3xy，y=2当x=0，求y。

解：dy/y=3x dx；lny=1.5x²+C；C=ln2，所以y=2e^(1.5x²)。

Cambridge-style application

Why is an arbitrary constant necessary?

Solution: Differentiation removes constants; integration represents a family of solutions until an initial condition selects one.

第 7 课｜复数｜Complex numbers

概念拆解｜Concept and meaning

i²=−1；Argand 图把z=x+iy视为点；模与辐角给极形式。

Detailed English explanation. Direction and order matter. A transformation must be described with every defining parameter; vector subtraction gives directed displacement; matrix multiplication acts in a fixed order; complex modulus and argument encode size and direction.

原理与方法｜Reasoning and method

共轭用于实化分母；乘法在极形式中模相乘、辐角相加。

例题一｜Worked example

化简 (3+2i)/(1−i)。

解：乘(1+i)：[(3+2i)(1+i)]/2=(1+5i)/2。

Cambridge-style application

Find modulus and argument of 1+i.

Solution: |z|=√2 and arg z=π/4.

第 9 课｜数值积分｜Numerical integration

概念拆解｜Concept and meaning

梯形法用直线段近似曲线下面积，分割越细通常越准确。

Detailed English explanation. Calculus links local change with accumulated change. Identify the inner function or variable, retain the constant of integration when required, and interpret derivatives, stationary points and definite integrals rather than treating them as symbol manipulation.

原理与方法｜Reasoning and method

h=(b−a)/n；端点一次、中间点两次。

例题一｜Worked example

用n=2梯形估算∫₀² x²dx。

解：h=1，估算=1/2[0+2(1)+4]=3。

Cambridge-style application

State why this is an overestimate.

Solution: For a convex curve y=x², the chords lie above the curve.

第 10 课｜P3 综合策略｜P3 synthesis

概念拆解｜Concept and meaning

P3 题常让前一小问提供后一问可用的恒等式或分解。

Detailed English explanation. Translate between words, symbols, diagrams and numerical values. State assumptions, keep exact values during working, and test the final result against the original domain, scale and context.

原理与方法｜Reasoning and method

先读完整题，保留题设形式；每一步检查定义域、参数与精度。

例题一｜Worked example

已知分解后求广义积分时应先检查什么？

解：检查分母零点是否落在积分区间；若有，普通定积分可能不存在。

Cambridge-style application

How should exact answers be presented?

Solution: Keep π, e, logarithms and radicals exact unless a decimal accuracy is explicitly requested.
