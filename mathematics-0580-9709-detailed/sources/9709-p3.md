## 第 1 课｜多项式与部分分式｜Polynomials and partial fractions

### 深度展开与真题化应用（共 8 个知识点）

#### 知识点 01/08｜核心定义｜Core definition

因式定理、余式定理与多项式除法用于拆解高次式；部分分式把有理式化为可积分形式。 学习时先用自己的话重述定义，再指出它与相邻概念的区别；这样遇到改变表述的题目仍能识别本质。

**English explanation.** The factor theorem, polynomial division and partial fractions form one chain: factor the denominator, determine unknown numerators, verify the identity, and only then use the decomposition.

#### 知识点 02/08｜符号、公式与条件｜Notation, formulae and conditions

余式定理 p(a) 等于多项式除以 x-a 的余数；若 p(a)=0，则 x-a 是因式。部分分式前必须先确认分子次数低于分母。 公式前先写变量含义和允许范围，公式后检查所得值是否满足这些条件。

**English explanation.** State every parameter and condition before calculation; notation is part of the mathematical model. A formula earns meaning only when its symbols and assumptions are made explicit.

#### 知识点 03/08｜方法为什么成立｜Why the method works

先确认真分式；分母不同因子决定分子形式，重复因子需逐级列项。 不要把计算器按键当作证明：至少保留建立关系、代入和关键化简三层。

**English explanation.** Explain the mathematical relationship, not only the calculator sequence. A reader should be able to reproduce the result without guessing which operation was used.

#### 知识点 04/08｜多种表示之间的转换｜Connecting representations

把分母完全因式分解：不同一次因子用常数分子；重复因子逐次列 A/(x-a)+B/(x-a)^2；不可约二次因子使用线性分子。 若图形趋势、代数符号和数表不一致，应暂停并检查模型，而不是继续机械计算。

**English explanation.** Move deliberately between words, algebra, a graph, a table, a diagram or a probability distribution. Agreement between representations is a powerful error check.

#### 知识点 05/08｜高频边界与易错点｜Boundaries and misconceptions

代回原式或通分复核系数。积分前若区间含分母零点，必须检查积分是否存在，不能机械套部分分式。 Cambridge 常把这一错误设计成选择题干扰项，结构题则会在后续小问放大前一步错误。

**English explanation.** A valid method can still give an invalid answer if its domain, sign, unit, interval or model assumptions are ignored. Test the final answer in the original statement.

#### 知识点 06/08｜基础示范与完整复核｜Worked foundation and verification

分解 (5x+1)/[(x+1)(x−2)]。 **正确答案：** 令=A/(x+1)+B/(x−2)，得5x+1=A(x−2)+B(x+1)。代x=2得B=11/3；x=−1得A=4/3。 完成后逆向代回原条件，并说明答案的精度、单位、范围或概率意义。

**English explanation.** Substitute the result back into the original condition and check its size, sign, accuracy and meaning. Keep exact values until the final requested approximation.

#### 知识点 07/08｜英文推理与答题句型｜English reasoning language

可使用以下骨架组织答案：From the given information...；Using...；Substituting gives...；Therefore...；This is valid because...。其中 because 后必须写具体数学条件。

**English explanation.** The factor theorem, polynomial division and partial fractions form one chain: factor the denominator, determine unknown numerators, verify the identity, and only then use the decomposition. Use complete sentences to identify the model, justify the operation and interpret the conclusion.

#### 知识点 08/08｜真题化应用与答案｜Cambridge-style application

Find remainder when p(x)=x³−2x+5 is divided by x−2. **参考答案：** Remainder=p(2)=8−4+5=9. 不只核对最终数值，还要对照答案中使用的定义、条件、方法和结论是否齐全。

**English explanation.** A Cambridge-style response makes the decisive method visible. The correct conclusion here is: Remainder=p(2)=8−4+5=9.

## 第 2 课｜模、指数与对数深化｜Modulus, exponential and logarithmic functions

### 深度展开与真题化应用（共 8 个知识点）

#### 知识点 01/08｜核心定义｜Core definition

模函数图像可由反射负值部分得到；指数增长率与当前值成正比。 学习时先用自己的话重述定义，再指出它与相邻概念的区别；这样遇到改变表述的题目仍能识别本质。

**English explanation.** Modulus removes sign, logarithms require positive arguments, and exponential outputs are positive. These facts determine both the method and the admissible solution set.

#### 知识点 02/08｜符号、公式与条件｜Notation, formulae and conditions

|f(x)| 表示 f(x) 到0的距离；指数函数始终为正；对数的真数必须大于0。这三个定义域条件是方程验根的核心。 公式前先写变量含义和允许范围，公式后检查所得值是否满足这些条件。

**English explanation.** State every parameter and condition before calculation; notation is part of the mathematical model. A formula earns meaning only when its symbols and assumptions are made explicit.

#### 知识点 03/08｜方法为什么成立｜Why the method works

解方程要结合图像交点和定义域；换底处理不同底数。 不要把计算器按键当作证明：至少保留建立关系、代入和关键化简三层。

**English explanation.** Explain the mathematical relationship, not only the calculator sequence. A reader should be able to reproduce the result without guessing which operation was used.

#### 知识点 04/08｜多种表示之间的转换｜Connecting representations

模方程按 f(x)=a 与 f(x)=-a 分支；指数方程统一底数或取对数；对数方程先合并，再检查每个真数。 若图形趋势、代数符号和数表不一致，应暂停并检查模型，而不是继续机械计算。

**English explanation.** Move deliberately between words, algebra, a graph, a table, a diagram or a probability distribution. Agreement between representations is a powerful error check.

#### 知识点 05/08｜高频边界与易错点｜Boundaries and misconceptions

**English explanation.** A valid method can still give an invalid answer if its domain, sign, unit, interval or model assumptions are ignored. Test the final answer in the original statement.

#### 知识点 06/08｜基础示范与完整复核｜Worked foundation and verification

**English explanation.** Substitute the result back into the original condition and check its size, sign, accuracy and meaning. Keep exact values until the final requested approximation.

#### 知识点 07/08｜英文推理与答题句型｜English reasoning language

**English explanation.** Modulus removes sign, logarithms require positive arguments, and exponential outputs are positive. These facts determine both the method and the admissible solution set. Use complete sentences to identify the model, justify the operation and interpret the conclusion.

#### 知识点 08/08｜真题化应用与答案｜Cambridge-style application

Solve |x²−4|=3. **参考答案：** x²−4=3 gives x=±√7; x²−4=−3 gives x=±1. 不只核对最终数值，还要对照答案中使用的定义、条件、方法和结论是否齐全。

## 第 4 课｜复杂微分｜Further differentiation

### 深度展开与真题化应用（共 8 个知识点）

#### 知识点 01/08｜核心定义｜Core definition

隐函数、参数方程和二阶导数扩展变化率分析。 学习时先用自己的话重述定义，再指出它与相邻概念的区别；这样遇到改变表述的题目仍能识别本质。

**English explanation.** Implicit and parametric differentiation preserve the dependency between variables. Every differentiated y-term carries dy/dx, and parametric gradients are ratios of rates.

#### 知识点 02/08｜符号、公式与条件｜Notation, formulae and conditions

隐函数中 y 是 x 的函数，因此微分 y² 得 2y(dy/dx)。参数方程则分别求 dx/dt 与 dy/dt，再相除。 公式前先写变量含义和允许范围，公式后检查所得值是否满足这些条件。

**English explanation.** State every parameter and condition before calculation; notation is part of the mathematical model. A formula earns meaning only when its symbols and assumptions are made explicit.

#### 知识点 03/08｜方法为什么成立｜Why the method works

隐式微分每个y项乘dy/dx；参数式dy/dx=(dy/dt)/(dx/dt)。 不要把计算器按键当作证明：至少保留建立关系、代入和关键化简三层。

**English explanation.** Explain the mathematical relationship, not only the calculator sequence. A reader should be able to reproduce the result without guessing which operation was used.

#### 知识点 04/08｜多种表示之间的转换｜Connecting representations

二阶导可通过 d/dx(dy/dx) 获得；参数式二阶导要把 d/dt(dy/dx) 再除以 dx/dt。 若图形趋势、代数符号和数表不一致，应暂停并检查模型，而不是继续机械计算。

**English explanation.** Move deliberately between words, algebra, a graph, a table, a diagram or a probability distribution. Agreement between representations is a powerful error check.

#### 知识点 05/08｜高频边界与易错点｜Boundaries and misconceptions

dx/dt=0 时 dy/dx 的表达式可能无定义，对应竖直切线或需进一步检查，不能直接约去使其消失。 Cambridge 常把这一错误设计成选择题干扰项，结构题则会在后续小问放大前一步错误。
