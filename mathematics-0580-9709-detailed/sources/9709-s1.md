## 第 1 课｜数据表示｜Representation of data

### 深度展开与真题化应用（共 8 个知识点）

#### 知识点 01/08｜核心定义｜Core definition

图表选择取决于数据类型；箱线图强调中位数与四分位，茎叶图保留原始值。 学习时先用自己的话重述定义，再指出它与相邻概念的区别；这样遇到改变表述的题目仍能识别本质。

**English explanation.** A statistical diagram is an encoding of data. Read what height, area, position and spread represent before making comparisons, and support every comparison with values.

#### 知识点 02/08｜符号、公式与条件｜Notation, formulae and conditions

原始数据、茎叶图、箱线图、直方图和累计频数图保留的信息不同。选择图形前先判断数据是离散还是连续、是否分组以及组距是否相等。 公式前先写变量含义和允许范围，公式后检查所得值是否满足这些条件。

**English explanation.** State every parameter and condition before calculation; notation is part of the mathematical model. A formula earns meaning only when its symbols and assumptions are made explicit.

#### 知识点 03/08｜方法为什么成立｜Why the method works

比较两组必须同时谈位置与离散，并联系情境。 不要把计算器按键当作证明：至少保留建立关系、代入和关键化简三层。

**English explanation.** Explain the mathematical relationship, not only the calculator sequence. A reader should be able to reproduce the result without guessing which operation was used.

#### 知识点 04/08｜多种表示之间的转换｜Connecting representations

中位数和IQR抗极端值；均值和标准差使用全部数据。比较两组要同时写中心与离散，并把数值解释回情境。 若图形趋势、代数符号和数表不一致，应暂停并检查模型，而不是继续机械计算。

**English explanation.** Move deliberately between words, algebra, a graph, a table, a diagram or a probability distribution. Agreement between representations is a powerful error check.

#### 知识点 05/08｜高频边界与易错点｜Boundaries and misconceptions

直方图柱面积代表频数，柱高是 frequency density=frequency/class width。组距不等时绝不能直接以频数作高度。 Cambridge 常把这一错误设计成选择题干扰项，结构题则会在后续小问放大前一步错误。

**English explanation.** A valid method can still give an invalid answer if its domain, sign, unit, interval or model assumptions are ignored. Test the final answer in the original statement.

#### 知识点 06/08｜基础示范与完整复核｜Worked foundation and verification

A组median 42,IQR 8；B组median 39,IQR 15，比较。 **正确答案：** A typical value higher and results more consistent because its median is larger and IQR smaller. 完成后逆向代回原条件，并说明答案的精度、单位、范围或概率意义。

**English explanation.** Substitute the result back into the original condition and check its size, sign, accuracy and meaning. Keep exact values until the final requested approximation.

#### 知识点 07/08｜英文推理与答题句型｜English reasoning language

可使用以下骨架组织答案：From the given information...；Using...；Substituting gives...；Therefore...；This is valid because...。其中 because 后必须写具体数学条件。

**English explanation.** A statistical diagram is an encoding of data. Read what height, area, position and spread represent before making comparisons, and support every comparison with values. Use complete sentences to identify the model, justify the operation and interpret the conclusion.

#### 知识点 08/08｜真题化应用与答案｜Cambridge-style application

Why is a histogram different from a bar chart? **参考答案：** Histogram bars touch and area represents frequency for continuous grouped data. 不只核对最终数值，还要对照答案中使用的定义、条件、方法和结论是否齐全。

**English explanation.** A Cambridge-style response makes the decisive method visible. The correct conclusion here is: Histogram bars touch and area represents frequency for continuous grouped data.

## 第 3 课｜概率规则｜Probability rules

### 深度展开与真题化应用（共 8 个知识点）

#### 知识点 01/08｜核心定义｜Core definition

加法法则处理OR，乘法/条件概率处理AND；独立与互斥绝不能混淆。 学习时先用自己的话重述定义，再指出它与相邻概念的区别；这样遇到改变表述的题目仍能识别本质。

**English explanation.** Probability notation translates logical words: intersection means “and”, union means inclusive “or”, and conditioning replaces the original sample space with a restricted one.

#### 知识点 02/08｜符号、公式与条件｜Notation, formulae and conditions

样本空间必须明确。互斥表示不能同时发生；独立表示一个事件不改变另一个概率，两者概念不同。 公式前先写变量含义和允许范围，公式后检查所得值是否满足这些条件。

**English explanation.** State every parameter and condition before calculation; notation is part of the mathematical model. A formula earns meaning only when its symbols and assumptions are made explicit.

#### 知识点 03/08｜方法为什么成立｜Why the method works

P(A∪B)=P(A)+P(B)−P(A∩B)。 不要把计算器按键当作证明：至少保留建立关系、代入和关键化简三层。

**English explanation.** Explain the mathematical relationship, not only the calculator sequence. A reader should be able to reproduce the result without guessing which operation was used.

#### 知识点 04/08｜多种表示之间的转换｜Connecting representations

P(A∪B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A∩B)；条件概率P(A|B)=P(A∩B)/P(B)。树图沿枝相乘、互斥路径相加。 若图形趋势、代数符号和数表不一致，应暂停并检查模型，而不是继续机械计算。

**English explanation.** Move deliberately between words, algebra, a graph, a table, a diagram or a probability distribution. Agreement between representations is a powerful error check.

#### 知识点 05/08｜高频边界与易错点｜Boundaries and misconceptions

不放回抽样通常改变后续概率；若声称独立，应验证P(A∩B)=P(A)P(B)，不能凭直觉。 Cambridge 常把这一错误设计成选择题干扰项，结构题则会在后续小问放大前一步错误。

**English explanation.** A valid method can still give an invalid answer if its domain, sign, unit, interval or model assumptions are ignored. Test the final answer in the original statement.

#### 知识点 06/08｜基础示范与完整复核｜Worked foundation and verification

**English explanation.** Substitute the result back into the original condition and check its size, sign, accuracy and meaning. Keep exact values until the final requested approximation.

#### 知识点 07/08｜英文推理与答题句型｜English reasoning language

可使用以下骨架组织答案：From the given information...；Using...；Substituting gives...；Therefore...；This is valid because...。其中 because 后必须写具体数学条件。

**English explanation.** Probability notation translates logical words: intersection means “and”, union means inclusive “or”, and conditioning replaces the original sample space with a restricted one. Use complete sentences to identify the model, justify the operation and interpret the conclusion.

#### 知识点 08/08｜真题化应用与答案｜Cambridge-style application

## 第 4 课｜离散随机变量｜Discrete random variables

### 深度展开与真题化应用（共 8 个知识点）

#### 知识点 01/08｜核心定义｜Core definition

概率分布列出X每个可能值及概率；期望是长期平均，不一定是可出现值。 学习时先用自己的话重述定义，再指出它与相邻概念的区别；这样遇到改变表述的题目仍能识别本质。

**English explanation.** Expectation is a probability-weighted balance point, while variance measures spread in squared units. They summarise a distribution but do not replace the full probability model.

#### 知识点 02/08｜符号、公式与条件｜Notation, formulae and conditions

随机变量把随机结果映射为数值。分布表必须列出所有可能值，概率非负且总和为1。 公式前先写变量含义和允许范围，公式后检查所得值是否满足这些条件。

**English explanation.** State every parameter and condition before calculation; notation is part of the mathematical model. A formula earns meaning only when its symbols and assumptions are made explicit.

#### 知识点 03/08｜方法为什么成立｜Why the method works

Σp=1；E(X)=Σxp；Var(X)=E(X²)−[E(X)]²。 不要把计算器按键当作证明：至少保留建立关系、代入和关键化简三层。

**English explanation.** Explain the mathematical relationship, not only the calculator sequence. A reader should be able to reproduce the result without guessing which operation was used.

#### 知识点 04/08｜多种表示之间的转换｜Connecting representations

E(X)=Σxp 是长期平均；Var(X)=E(X²)-[E(X)]² 衡量围绕均值的平方离差。 若图形趋势、代数符号和数表不一致，应暂停并检查模型，而不是继续机械计算。

**English explanation.** Move deliberately between words, algebra, a graph, a table, a diagram or a probability distribution. Agreement between representations is a powerful error check.

#### 知识点 05/08｜高频边界与易错点｜Boundaries and misconceptions

E(X)不必是X可能取到的值；方差不能为负。线性变换满足E(aX+b)=aE(X)+b，Var(aX+b)=a²Var(X)。 Cambridge 常把这一错误设计成选择题干扰项，结构题则会在后续小问放大前一步错误。

**English explanation.** A valid method can still give an invalid answer if its domain, sign, unit, interval or model assumptions are ignored. Test the final answer in the original statement.

#### 知识点 06/08｜基础示范与完整复核｜Worked foundation and verification

#### 知识点 01/08｜核心定义｜Core definition

先识别模型条件，再计算；最终概率必须在0到1之间。 学习时先用自己的话重述定义，再指出它与相邻概念的区别；这样遇到改变表述的题目仍能识别本质。

**English explanation.** A complete statistics solution names the random variable, justifies the model, states parameters and event, calculates transparently, and interprets the final probability.

#### 知识点 02/08｜符号、公式与条件｜Notation, formulae and conditions

综合题先判断数据类型、事件结构与分布条件，再选择统计量或概率模型。计算器指令不能替代模型说明。 公式前先写变量含义和允许范围，公式后检查所得值是否满足这些条件。

#### 知识点 04/08｜多种表示之间的转换｜Connecting representations

**English explanation.** Move deliberately between words, algebra, a graph, a table, a diagram or a probability distribution. Agreement between representations is a powerful error check.

#### 知识点 05/08｜高频边界与易错点｜Boundaries and misconceptions

**English explanation.** A valid method can still give an invalid answer if its domain, sign, unit, interval or model assumptions are ignored. Test the final answer in the original statement.
