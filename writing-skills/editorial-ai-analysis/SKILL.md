# AI 产业分析文章风格

Use this skill when the deliverable is a Chinese analytical article intended for public reading, especially on AI, Agent engineering, open source, technical ecosystems, or innovation organizations.

Before drafting or materially revising, read [the style and release guide](references/style-and-release-guide.md). It records the user's approved visual language, chart standards, narrative posture, evidence discipline, and public-release checks.

## Working approach

- Start from the primary material where possible: an original talk, interview, project repository, paper, or official announcement. Distinguish a speaker's assertion, independent evidence, and the article's inference in the wording.
- Produce a standalone HTML article by default. Keep a readable source list with direct links and state any uncertainty that meaningfully affects the conclusion.
- Use diagrams to make a relationship, mechanism, trade-off, or timeline easier to understand. Do not add decorative AI art merely to fill space.
- Preserve the article's point of view: make a clear, testable judgment, then show its limits and practical implications. Avoid empty exhortation, slogan-only conclusions, and inflated news language.
- When the user asks for a DOCX/PDF, follow the relevant document/PDF skill in addition to this style guide.

## Boundaries

- Do not turn a source's product metrics, capital news, performance claim, or motivation into an established fact without independent support; attribute it precisely if only a source supports it.
- Do not identify private individuals through a combination of city, school, employer, customer, money, or career details. Remove editorial process notes from publishable prose.
- For public-facing drafts, perform the release check in the guide. It should improve clarity and factual caution, not flatten the article into generic corporate language.
